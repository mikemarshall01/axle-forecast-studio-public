# Decision 0003: provisional finance display

Status: accepted product choice from Mike, 26 September 2026. Commercial owner/sign fixtures remain pending.

Until each relevant commercial amount has a verified payer, payee, sign, accounting boundary, rate and baseline, the dashboard shows separate **illustrative** route and stakeholder components. It does not publish an aggregate labelled “Axle cash”, “portfolio cash” or an equivalent net total. Missing commercial terms are **unavailable**, not zero.

Physical energy, customer bill effects, supplier benefit, Capacity Market accrual, cash transfers and terminal inventory value remain distinct. The model may calculate transparent fixture-local examples for tests, but those are not actual revenue or settlement claims.

An aggregate may be introduced only for a defined set of components whose economic owners and signs have been approved and whose cash entries reconcile without double counting. The inclusion rule and limitations must be visible in code, tests, exports and the dashboard.
