# Decision 0006: illustrative supplier P&L

Status: accepted by the lead on 29 September 2026 under Mike's approval of the Supplier P&L view (decision 0004 item 62) and his instruction to proceed on the lead's recommendations. Amends decision 0003 for one defined set of components. Listed in `docs/plans/2026-09-30-overnight-review-log.md` for Mike's review.

The Supplier page may show a net "illustrative supplier gain" per treated customer per month, never "Axle cash" or a real supplier margin. It is the sum of four defined components, each per simulated week, from the supplier's point of view (positive = gain): energy procurement saving at the day-ahead price (equal to minus the `cost_effect` home-import difference), hedge-error saving at (imbalance − day-ahead) against a like-for-like profiled hedge (smart hedge = warm-up baseline minus the aggregator's forecast shift), third-party grid-event payments, and the customer payment (negative; an editable share of the supplier's weekly positive gross, or a flat amount per customer that is unset by default). An optional platform fee is unavailable (NaN), not zero, unless set; the headline is the before-fee figure.

Assumptions shown with the figure: a flat (non-time-of-use) retail tariff, so the supplier keeps the procurement saving (the customer-value frames take the pass-through reading instead, and the two are never added); the supplier hedges the aggregator's full forecast and does not re-hedge intraday; control-group EVs are outside the treated count. The trading ledger (decision 0005) is shown beside the supplier P&L and never added, because both value the same shifted energy. Every price and volume is synthetic.
